No. 02 — Seasonal

Strawberry Rhubarb

We roast the fruit together before it goes anywhere near the churn. The rhubarb collapses, the sugars concentrate, and the acidity holds instead of flattening out the way it does when you stir raw fruit into a finished base. It runs until the rhubarb does.
