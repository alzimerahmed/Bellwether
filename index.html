Questions

Frequently Asked

What flavors does Bellwether Creamery offer?

Bellwether Creamery keeps sixteen flavors in rotation at all times. The range includes pints, bars, sandwiches, dairy-free oat-base pints, fruit sorbets, and seasonal limited batches. Seasonal flavors change based on what local growers have available.

Do you have dairy-free ice cream?

Yes. Bellwether Creamery churns dairy-free options on a separate line with its own equipment. The creamy dairy-free pints use an oat base, and the sorbets are made with water, fruit, or cocoa. No dairy cross-contamination.

Where are your scoop shops located?

Bellwether Creamery has three scoop shops: Old Mill at 41 Mill Race Lane (the original kitchen, open 11am to 10pm), Riverside at 208 Towpath Street (open 12pm to 11pm with patio seating), and Harbor Green at 7 Quayside Row (open 12pm to 9pm).

Do you ship ice cream?

Yes. Bellwether Creamery ships overnight with dry ice so pints arrive solid. Shipping is free on orders over $60, otherwise a flat $8.50. Same-day local delivery is available within eight miles of the Old Mill kitchen for orders placed before 2pm.

How is your ice cream made?

Bellwether Creamery churns two gallons at a time every morning. The process starts with steeping ingredients in cream overnight, then slow churning cold to minimize air, and finally folding mix-ins by hand with a paddle so ripples stay in streaks and shards.

What is small-batch ice cream?

Small-batch ice cream is churned in limited quantities so one person can taste everything that goes out, adjust salt in the next run, and pull anything that is not right before it reaches a counter. Bellwether Creamery makes two gallons at a time, which is small by commercial standards.
